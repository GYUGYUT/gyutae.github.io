Research Projects

UniPrompt-CL: Single-Inference Domain Continual Learning · WACV 2027, first author
  • Replaced per-layer prompt pools with one pool shared by all ViT layers, so prompt selection and prediction take a single backbone pass; no past data stored.
  • Diabetic retinopathy: +2.0%p over the strongest same-backbone method, lowest forgetting (AvgF 0.079), training cost ~¼ of CODA-Prompt (44.17 GFLOPs).
2025.09 – 2026.10
Multimodal Diagnosis of Exposure Keratopathy & Infectious Keratitis · with Samsung Medical Center
  • Led all stages except clinical data collection (4-person team): design, modeling, experiments, papers, KR/PCT patents.
  • Contrastive alignment of four illumination modalities into the broad-beam image at training time; single-image inference reaches accuracy 0.841 / F1 0.838 (+16%p vs. single-modality training).
  • Single ViT with mask-aware cross-attention for any 1–4 image combination: 3-class accuracy 0.785 → 0.835, macro AUROC 0.964 (333 patients, 2,413 sets, 10 repeats).
2024.10 – 2026.09
NRF Core Research: Adaptive State Inference from Partially Available Multimodal Data
  • ER/ICU deterioration prediction from incomplete ECG, vital-sign, and lab data. One of two core Ph.D. student researchers; managing development by five M.S. researchers.
2026.09 – (3 yrs)
Co-authored research (method advisor) · ACCV 2026
  • Spectral Descriptor Conditioning: proposed the 2D polar frequency descriptor at the core of the paper — best average PSNR 30.07 dB over five degradations with only 2.2% extra FLOPs.
  • Drift-adaptive Representation Preservation: co-designed drift-scaled preservation — 3.0 GB training memory (4.7–10.7× less) and 13.78 MB storage.
2026.03 – 2026.09
PatchCore++: Noise-Robust Industrial Anomaly Detection · ICCE-Asia 2024, first author
